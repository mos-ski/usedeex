# CEO Welcome Email Revision

## Goal

Replace the current slogan-heavy welcome email with a warm, credible note that reads as though it was personally written by Omojuwa Divine, CEO of UseDeeX.

## Content

The email will use this copy:

> Hi {{name}},
>
> I’m Omojuwa Divine, CEO of UseDeeX. I wanted to personally welcome you to DeeX.
>
> We built DeeX to make managing your money simpler—whether you’re holding or exchanging digital assets, sending money, paying bills, buying gift cards, or earning rewards.
>
> Your account is ready, and you can start exploring whenever you’re ready. If anything feels unclear or you need help, our team is always here for you.
>
> Thank you for choosing DeeX. We’re glad to have you with us.
>
> Welcome aboard,
>
> Omojuwa Divine  
> CEO, UseDeeX

The CTA label will be `Explore DeeX`.

## Visual Treatment

- Preserve the approved DeeX email shell, hero artwork, footer, and centered layout.
- Use the brand blue only for the welcome headline.
- Render every body paragraph and both signature lines in the same existing brand grey.
- Keep comfortable vertical spacing so the message reads like a personal letter rather than a receipt or promotional banner.

## Scope

Only the welcome email changes. Other operational templates and the preview workspace remain unchanged.

## Verification

- Update the welcome template test to assert the new CEO copy, CTA, centered alignment, and uniform grey body treatment.
- Run the welcome, catalogue, and preview tests.
- Run the production build and visually inspect the local preview before publishing the update.
