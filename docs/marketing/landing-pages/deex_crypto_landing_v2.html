help_outline FAQ

Questions before
you trade?

How long does it take to receive Naira?expand_more
Most trades complete in under 5 minutes from the moment your crypto arrives at the deposit address. Bitcoin may take slightly longer due to network confirmations — typically 1 to 3 blocks.
Is the rate I see the rate I actually get?expand_more
Yes. When you initiate a sell, the rate is locked for a time window. As long as your crypto arrives within that window, you get exactly the rate shown — no surprises.
Do I need to fund a DeeX wallet first?expand_more
No. You can sell directly from an external wallet. Enter your amount, get the deposit address, send crypto from your own wallet, and Naira goes straight to your bank.
Which networks are supported?expand_more
BTC supports Bitcoin Mainnet and Lightning. ETH supports ERC-20. USDT supports TRC-20, ERC-20, and BEP-20. SOL supports Solana Mainnet. Always confirm the network before sending — wrong network transfers cannot be recovered.
When does the ₦50,000 bonus unlock?expand_more
The ₦50,000 credit is issued after your first completed trade. It can be used toward bill payments, swap fees, or future transactions. Not available as direct cash withdrawal. Valid 30 days from account creation.
Is DeeX safe?expand_more
DeeX uses 3-tier KYC, BVN encryption, and PIN-protected withdrawals. Your funds are tracked individually — not held in a shared pool. We do not store payment credentials.
