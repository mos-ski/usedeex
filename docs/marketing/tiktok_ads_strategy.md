## 1. Campaign Setup

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---------|----------------|
| **Objective** | App Promotion → App Install |
| **Buying Type** | Auction (lowest cost) |
| **Budget** | Start with ₦100,000–₦300,000/day, scale winners |
| **Optimization** | Optimize for "App Install" first, then create retargeting for "Sign Up" |

## 2. Targeting Strategy

### Primary Audience

| Parameter | Targeting |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Location** | Nigeria (All states, prioritize Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano) |
| **Age** | 18–45 |
| **Gender** | All |
| **Device** | Android (primary), iOS |
| **Interests** | Cryptocurrency, Bitcoin, Investing, Gift cards, Online shopping, Mobile banking, Betting/sports, Fintech |

### Custom Audiences

- App install but no signup → Retarget with KYC ease messaging
- Website visitors (if you have a landing page)
- Lookalike based on existing users (once you have data)

### Behaviors

- Mobile payment app users
- Finance app installers (last 30 days)

## 3. Ad Group Structure

Create 3–4 ad groups with different hooks:

| Ad Group | Angle | Hook |
|----------|-------|------|
| **Crypto Traders** | "Beat bank rates" | "Sell your Bitcoin at the best Naira rate" |
| **Gift Card Sellers** | "Instant cash" | "Turn unused gift cards to Naira in 2 minutes" |
| **Bill Payments** | "No more delays" | "Pay PHCN, buy airtime, fund betting — all in one app" |
| **Virtual Cards** | "Shop globally" | "Get a USD virtual Visa card — no domiciliary account needed" |

## 4. Creative Strategy (TikTok Native)

### Hook 0–3 seconds (Critical)

- "Stop selling your crypto to scammers!"
- "I just got ₦50,000 for my Apple gift card instantly"
- "This app pays your Nepa bill in 10 seconds"
- Text overlay: "₦10,000 giveaway for new users"

### Content Types

1. **UGC-style testimonial:** Nigerian creator showing phone screen, walking through app
2. **Before/After:** "How I used to sell crypto" vs "How I sell crypto now with DeeX"
3. **Speed demo:** Timed challenge — "Let's pay for electricity in under 30 seconds"
4. **Referral showcase:** "I made ₦20,000 just sharing my referral link"
5. **Trending audio:** Use Nigerian sounds + finance meme formats

### Call-to-Action

- "Link in bio to download"
- "Click download button"

## 6. Tracking & Attribution

### Required Setup

1. **TikTok App Events SDK** — Install SDK in your Android/iOS app

### App Event Mapping

| Event | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `App Install` | Install |
| `App Launch` | Open |
| `Registration` | Sign Up (Primary conversion) |
| `Purchase` | First trade/bill payment |
| `Complete KYC` | Level 1/2/3 verification |

### Deep Linking

Use deferred deep links to send users to specific onboarding screens post-install.

## 7. Landing Experience

### Option A: Direct App Install

Link to Google Play Store / App Store. Optimize app store listing with screenshots showing:
- Crypto rates
- Gift card rates
- Bill payment UI
- Rewards dashboard

### Option B: Pre-Landing Page (Recommended)

Mobile-optimized landing page with:
- **Headline:** "Nigeria's All-in-One Crypto & Payment App"
- **Social proof:** "Join 2,800+ Nigerians trading on DeeX"
- **Promo:** "Get 100 DeeXPoints (₦1,000) when you sign up"
- **CTAs:** "Download on Play Store" / "Download on App Store"

---

## 8. Budget Allocation

| Phase | Duration | Budget | Focus |
|-------|----------|--------|-------|
| **Testing** | Week 1–2 | ₦200K–₦500K | 4–6 creative variations, find winning hooks |
| **Scaling** | Week 3–4 | ₦500K–₦1M | Double down on 2–3 winning ads |
| **Retention** | Ongoing | ₦300K/week | Retargeting installs → signups → first trade |

---

## 9. Key Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Install Rate | >2% |
| Cost Per Install (CPI) | <₦200 |
| Signup Rate (install → signup) | >30% |
| Cost Per Signup | <₦600 |
| KYC Completion Rate | >20% |
| First Trade Rate | >10% |

## 10. Compliance & Trust Signals

Since you're in fintech, add trust elements:
- "Licensed by CBN" (if applicable)
- "Your BVN is secure — encrypted & verified"
- Show security badges in creatives
- Mention "3-tier KYC for your protection"

## 11. Sample Ad Script

**Hook:** "POV: You finally found a legit app to sell your Bitcoin in Nigeria"

**Script:**
- (0–3s) Text: "No more WhatsApp traders"
- (3–8s) Screen recording of app → Sell BTC → Get ₦ rate instantly
- (8–15s) "I sold $500 BTC and got ₦750,000 in my account in 5 minutes"
- (15–20s) "Plus they have gift cards, bill payments, even virtual dollar cards"
- (CTA) "Download DeeX now — link in bio"
