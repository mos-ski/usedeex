# AML/CFT Policy & Risk Assessment

**Entity:** DeeX Technologies Ltd (DEEX OPTIONS LTD)
**Date:** 2026-06-22

## 1. Purpose

DeeX maintains a risk-based AML/CFT program to identify, assess, and mitigate money laundering and terrorism financing (ML/TF) risk across its products: crypto/digital asset trading, gift card exchange, bill payments, P2P transfers, virtual cards, and merchant payments.

## 2. How We Assess Risk

We rate ML/TF risk across four dimensions and apply controls proportional to the rating:

| Dimension | What We Look At |
|---|---|
| Customer risk | KYC tier, transaction behavior, declared source of income, PEP/sanctions status |
| Product risk | Higher inherent risk on crypto trading and gift cards (conversion/layering vectors); lower on bill payments |
| Channel risk | Fully digital, non-face-to-face onboarding |
| Geographic risk | Cross-border counterparties, exposure to high-risk jurisdictions |

Customers are scored against these factors at onboarding and on an ongoing basis; higher-risk customers are subject to Enhanced Due Diligence (additional source-of-funds evidence and senior compliance sign-off before limit increases).

## 3. Sanctions & PEP Screening

Customers are screened against sanctions lists and PEP databases at onboarding, with periodic re-screening for active customers. A confirmed sanctions match results in immediate account restriction; a PEP match triggers Enhanced Due Diligence.

## 4. Transaction Monitoring

DeeX runs an internal Compliance Engine that automatically flags transaction patterns for review:

| Alert | Triggers On |
|---|---|
| High-frequency trading | Trade velocity inconsistent with the customer's profile |
| Multi-device usage | Abnormal number of distinct devices on one account |
| Large withdrawal | Withdrawals at or near tier limits, or repeated near-limit patterns |
| Failed KYC | Repeated failed or inconsistent KYC submissions |
| Wash trading | Circular trading with no genuine economic purpose |

Flagged accounts are triaged by a Compliance Officer (pending → reviewing → resolved/dismissed) and can be cleared, dismissed, or suspended. Every action is logged in an internal audit trail.

## 5. Suspicious Activity Reporting

Activity confirmed as suspicious is escalated internally to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO), who is responsible for filing a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) with the Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU) within the regulatory timeframe.

## 6. Recordkeeping & Review

Transaction records, alert dispositions, and screening results are retained for a minimum of 5 years. This risk assessment is reviewed at least annually, or upon a new product launch or material regulatory change.
