# KYC Policy & Procedures

**Entity:** DeeX Technologies Ltd (DEEX OPTIONS LTD)
**Date:** 2026-06-22

## 1. Purpose

DeeX operates a tiered Know Your Customer (KYC) program to verify customer identity before granting access to trading and withdrawal functionality, in line with CBN tiered-KYC guidance. Every customer is verified before they can transact, and access expands as verification deepens.

## 2. Tiered Verification Levels

| Tier | What We Collect | Trading Limit | Withdrawal Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 1** | Bank Verification Number (BVN) + liveness selfie | $1,000 | $100 (one-time) |
| **Level 2** | Government-issued ID (NIN, passport, or driver's license) + proof of address (≤3 months old) | $10,000 | $500 (daily) |
| **Level 3** | Employment/business documentation, declared source of income, risk questionnaire, mandatory 2FA | $25,000 | $1,000 (daily) |

A customer cannot exceed the limits of their current tier, and cannot advance to the next tier until the current one is reviewed and approved.

## 3. Verification Process

1. **Identity capture** — BVN is collected and checked against NIBSS; a liveness selfie confirms the applicant is a real, present person (anti-spoofing).
2. **Document verification** — Government ID and proof of address are reviewed to confirm legal identity, age, and residential address.
3. **Profile & risk questionnaire** — At Level 3, the customer declares employment/business status, source of income, and expected transaction volume, and enables 2FA.
4. **Compliance review** — Every submission is reviewed by a Compliance Officer through an internal KYC queue and explicitly approved or rejected (with reason) before any limit increase takes effect.
5. **Re-verification** — Documents are refreshed if they expire, or if proof of address ages past the 3-month freshness window at the next tier upgrade.

## 4. Recordkeeping

KYC submissions, supporting documents, and all approval/rejection decisions are retained for the life of the customer relationship plus a minimum 5-year regulatory retention period, and are recorded in an internal audit log.

## 5. Ownership

The Compliance function is responsible for reviewing KYC submissions and maintaining this process; the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) has overall accountability for the program.
